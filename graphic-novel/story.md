# LAST STOP AT DAWN
## Issue 01 — The Envelope
**Genre:** Contemporary urban mystery  
**Setting:** London, 04:13 AM, on the final night of the old number 17 night-bus route  
**Format:** Four-page proof-of-concept, designed to expand into a 24-page first issue  
**Originality note:** Original characters, plot, dialogue, and visual direction for this project.

## Logline
On the last night of the old number 17 bus route, night-shift cleaner Mara Venn finds a paper envelope connecting three strangers — and a disappearance the city has quietly agreed to forget.

## Main cast
- **Mara Venn (26):** A night-shift cleaner who trusts routines more than people. As a child, she was photographed at the centre of a missing-person case she cannot remember.
- **The Stranger:** A guarded person who has carried an old photograph for seventeen years. They know Mara's name and what was hidden from her.
- **The Driver:** The last driver of route 17, who has seen the same passenger board at the same stop every anniversary.

## Issue 01 — page script

### PAGE 1 — THE ROUTE ENDS HERE (2 panels)
**Panel 1 — Wide establishing shot.** Rain stripes a quiet London street. The final red number 17 bus idles under sodium streetlights. Caption: “04:13 AM · LAST SERVICE. THE CITY ISN'T ASLEEP.”

**Panel 2 — Medium.** Mara, wearing a worn reflective jacket, finds a sealed envelope beside the bus shelter bench. She turns it over. Mara: “Who leaves a letter here?”

### PAGE 2 — A NAME FROM THE PAST (3 panels)
**Panel 1 — Insert close-up.** The envelope is addressed to MARA VENN. The handwritten note reads: “IF YOU'RE READING THIS, THEY'VE CLOSED THE FILE.” A faded date stamp reads 2009.

**Panel 2 — Medium close-up.** Mara reads under the bus stop light. Mara: “This was written seventeen years ago.”

**Panel 3 — Wide.** A second passenger watches from the far end of the shelter, half-hidden in shadow. Caption: “SOMEONE ELSE KNOWS WHAT'S IN THAT ENVELOPE.”

### PAGE 3 — THE STRANGER KNOWS HER NAME (3 panels)
**Panel 1 — Tall two-shot.** The Stranger steps into the light and holds out a hand. Stranger: “You shouldn't have opened that, Mara.”

**Panel 2 — Close-up.** The Stranger reaches for the envelope. Stranger: “Give it to me. I'll tell you why.”

**Panel 3 — Medium.** Mara pulls it back, refusing to move. Mara: “Start by telling me who disappeared.”

### PAGE 4 — THE ENVELOPE (3 panels)
**Panel 1 — Insert.** The Stranger reveals an old photograph of a little girl beside the number 17 bus. On the back: “MARA · 2009.”

**Panel 2 — Close-up on Mara.** Her expression shifts from suspicion to shock. Mara: “That girl is me. I was nine.”

**Panel 3 — Wide final image.** The final bus pulls away as dawn begins to break. The Stranger remains under the shelter light. Caption: “TO BE CONTINUED. THE REAL STORY STARTS AT DAWN.”

## Art direction
- Cinematic, grounded London atmosphere; wet pavements, bus-shelter glass, sodium amber light and deep blue-grey shadows.
- Bold ink-like outlines, graphic shapes, restrained palette: midnight blue, brick red, warm paper, amber.
- Dialogue must be readable at phone size. Keep lettering separate from artwork wherever possible.
- Keep Mara's short dark hair, reflective jacket, and amber safety stripes consistent from panel to panel.
- No supernatural explanations. Mystery should be driven by people, memory, documents, and choices.

## Expansion plan
1. Pages 5–8: Mara questions the driver and discovers the envelope is part of a missing-person file.
2. Pages 9–16: The Stranger reveals a contradiction in the official timeline; someone starts following them.
3. Pages 17–22: Mara finds a witness who remembers the night differently.
4. Pages 23–24: A new piece of evidence reframes the disappearance and sets up Issue 02.
